About this project

The knot bag (sometimes called an azuma-style or knot wristlet) is a traditional Japanese shape: one long and one short handle. Thread the long handle through the short loop to close the bag, or carry it over your wrist. It's fully lined with every seam hidden, and it's a lovely introduction to sewing curves.

Techniques you'll use

  • Pre-washing fabric
  • Pressing
  • Backstitching
  • Seam allowances & right sides together
  • Topstitching
  • Lining a bag
  • Clipping corners & curves
  • Turning gap & ladder stitch
  • Sewing curves

Safety

  • Keep fingers clear of the needle; switch off before changing needles or feet.
  • Irons are hot — stand upright when not in use and keep cords tidy.
  • Close rotary cutters and keep scissors pointed down when walking.
