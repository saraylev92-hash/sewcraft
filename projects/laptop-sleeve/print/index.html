Print pattern · Padded Laptop Sleeve

  1. STEP 1

    Print the test page

    In the print dialog choose Actual size / 100% — never “Fit to page”.

  2. STEP 2

    Measure the squares

    The squares must measure exactly 5 cm and 2″. If not, check printer scaling and print again.

  3. STEP 3

    Print the pieces

    19 pages. Trim along the solid border where pages meet, then match the ◆ marks and tape.

Pieces to print

Pieces to print — all pieces are rectangles, so you can also measure them straight onto fabric.

Sample pattern — not yet test-sewn. Pattern rules: Width = laptop width + thickness + 2 cm padding ease + seam allowances. The length wraps front and back (2 × (depth + thickness + 1 cm)) plus a 12 cm flap. Measure the closed laptop at its largest point.
