About this project

Hemmed edges, straps and a patch pocket — skills that carry over into almost every other project. Sturdy cotton twill or canvas makes it last for years in the kitchen or workshop.

Techniques you'll use

  • Pre-washing fabric
  • Pressing
  • Edgestitching
  • Attaching straps
  • Double-fold hem

Safety

  • Keep fingers clear of the needle; switch off before changing needles or feet.
  • Irons are hot — stand upright when not in use and keep cords tidy.
  • Close rotary cutters and keep scissors pointed down when walking.
