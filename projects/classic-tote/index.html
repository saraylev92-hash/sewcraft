Project stages

About this project

This is the perfect first 'real' project: straight seams, a clean lined finish, and techniques you'll use again and again — boxed corners, strap making and topstitching. The canvas outer stands up on its own, and the size is fully adjustable before you cut.

Techniques you'll use

  • Pre-washing fabric
  • Pressing
  • Straight stitch
  • Backstitching
  • Seam allowances & right sides together
  • Edgestitching
  • Topstitching
  • Boxed corners
  • Lining a bag
  • Attaching straps
  • Clipping corners & curves
  • Turning gap & ladder stitch

Safety

  • Keep fingers clear of the needle; switch off before changing needles or feet.
  • Irons are hot — stand upright when not in use and keep cords tidy.
  • Close rotary cutters and keep scissors pointed down when walking.
