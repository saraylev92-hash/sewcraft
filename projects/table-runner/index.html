Project stages

About this project

Sew a top and a back together, turn, topstitch. Choose a seasonal print on one side and a plain on the other. Set it to the exact length of your table.

Techniques you'll use

  • Pre-washing fabric
  • Pressing
  • Backstitching
  • Seam allowances & right sides together
  • Topstitching
  • Clipping corners & curves

Safety

  • Keep fingers clear of the needle; switch off before changing needles or feet.
  • Irons are hot — stand upright when not in use and keep cords tidy.
  • Close rotary cutters and keep scissors pointed down when walking.

Size & customisation

Choose a size or set your own dimensions. Cutting sizes, fabric amounts and step measurements update together.
