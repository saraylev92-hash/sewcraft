Project stages

About this project

Napkins are the best way to master a perfect hem. Mitred corners lie flat and look professional, and once you've made four you'll never fear corners again. Choose your size; the hem allowance stays the same.

Techniques you'll use

  • Pre-washing fabric
  • Pressing
  • Edgestitching
  • Turning gap & ladder stitch
  • Double-fold hem

Safety

  • Keep fingers clear of the needle; switch off before changing needles or feet.
  • Irons are hot — stand upright when not in use and keep cords tidy.
  • Close rotary cutters and keep scissors pointed down when walking.
