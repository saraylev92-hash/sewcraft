About this project

A flared, curved-hem cape in any size from toddler to tween. It's lined so it swishes nicely, and the neck closes with hook-and-loop tape that pulls apart safely — never ties.

Techniques you'll use

  • Zigzag stitch
  • Edgestitching
  • Topstitching
  • Clipping corners & curves
  • Double-fold binding
  • Sewing curves

Safety

  • Keep fingers clear of the needle; switch off before changing needles or feet.
  • Irons are hot — stand upright when not in use and keep cords tidy.
  • Close rotary cutters and keep scissors pointed down when walking.
