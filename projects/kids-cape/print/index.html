Print pattern · Kids' Hero Cape

  1. STEP 1

    Print the test page

    In the print dialog choose Actual size / 100% — never “Fit to page”.

  2. STEP 2

    Measure the squares

    The squares must measure exactly 5 cm and 2″. If not, check printer scaling and print again.

  3. STEP 3

    Print the pieces

    29 pages. Trim along the solid border where pages meet, then match the ◆ marks and tape.

Pieces to print

Pieces to print — curved pieces must be printed; rectangles can also be measured straight onto fabric.

Kids' Hero Cape

SewCraft pattern · A4 · 11/17/2026

5 × 5 cm
2″ × 2″

Check scale: measure both squares with a ruler. If they are not exact, set printing to “Actual size” / 100% and reprint. Do not cut fabric from a mis-scaled print.

Fabric

  • Outer — satin or cotton: 0.7 m of 112 cm wide (single)
  • Lining — contrast cotton: 0.7 m of 112 cm wide (single)

Sample pattern — not yet test-sewn. Pattern rules: The cape flares from a fixed 26 cm neck edge to 1.2 × its length at the hem, with a gently curved hem. The neck band and closure stay the same at every size.
