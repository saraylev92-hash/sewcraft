Print pattern · Simple Elastic-Waist Skirt

  1. STEP 1

    Print the test page

    In the print dialog choose Actual size / 100% — never “Fit to page”.

  2. STEP 2

    Measure the squares

    The squares must measure exactly 5 cm and 2″. If not, check printer scaling and print again.

  3. STEP 3

    Print the pieces

    16 pages. Trim along the solid border where pages meet, then match the ◆ marks and tape.

Pieces to print

Pieces to print — all pieces are rectangles, so you can also measure them straight onto fabric.

Sample pattern — not yet test-sewn. Pattern rules: This pattern is drafted, not scaled: panel width comes from your hip × 1.5 fullness (minimum hip + 20 cm so it always pulls on), elastic from 90% of your waist plus a 2 cm overlap, and length from your chosen length plus fixed casing and hem allowances. Seam allowance stays 1.5 cm at every size. Size presets are typical body measurements — your own measurements are better.
