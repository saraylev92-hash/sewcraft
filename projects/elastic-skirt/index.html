Project stages

About this project

Two rectangles, an elastic casing and a hem — but drafted from your measurements so it actually fits. The panel width is based on your hip (so it always pulls on), the elastic on your waist, and the length is yours to choose.

Techniques you'll use

  • Pre-washing fabric
  • Pressing
  • Zigzag stitch
  • Seam allowances & right sides together
  • Making a casing
  • Elastic waistband
  • Double-fold hem
  • Finishing raw edges

Safety

  • Keep fingers clear of the needle; switch off before changing needles or feet.
  • Irons are hot — stand upright when not in use and keep cords tidy.
  • Close rotary cutters and keep scissors pointed down when walking.

Size & customisation

Choose a size or set your own dimensions. Cutting sizes, fabric amounts and step measurements update together.

How sizing works: This pattern is drafted, not scaled: panel width comes from your hip × 1.5 fullness (minimum hip + 20 cm so it always pulls on), elastic from 90% of your waist plus a 2 cm overlap, and length from your chosen length plus fixed casing and hem allowances. Seam allowance stays 1.5 cm at every size. Size presets are typical body measurements — your own measurements are better.
