About this project

The best first project for kids learning to sew with an adult: tiny seams, instant results. Use felt or cotton scraps and fill with rice or plastic pellets.

Techniques you'll use

  • Pressing
  • Backstitching
  • Seam allowances & right sides together
  • Clipping corners & curves
  • Turning gap & ladder stitch

Safety

  • Keep fingers clear of the needle; switch off before changing needles or feet.
  • Irons are hot — stand upright when not in use and keep cords tidy.
  • Close rotary cutters and keep scissors pointed down when walking.
