Reversible Pet Bandana

Project stages

About this project

My dog has one for every season! Sewn as a square, cut into triangles and turned; the top edge folds over to make a channel for the collar. Measure the collar and adjust the square.

Techniques you'll use

  • Topstitching
  • Clipping corners & curves

Safety

  • Keep fingers clear of the needle; switch off before changing needles or feet.
  • Irons are hot — stand upright when not in use and keep cords tidy.
  • Close rotary cutters and keep scissors pointed down when walking.
