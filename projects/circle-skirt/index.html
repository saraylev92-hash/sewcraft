Project stages

About this project

Four quarter-circle panels, drafted with real geometry: the waist radius comes from your hip measurement so the skirt pulls on, and an elastic band gathers it gently to your waist. You'll learn staystitching, sewing bias edges and levelling a hem.

Techniques you'll use

  • Pre-washing fabric
  • Pressing
  • Seam allowances & right sides together
  • Elastic waistband
  • Double-fold hem
  • Finishing raw edges
  • Sewing curves
  • Staystitching

Safety

  • Keep fingers clear of the needle; switch off before changing needles or feet.
  • Irons are hot — stand upright when not in use and keep cords tidy.
  • Close rotary cutters and keep scissors pointed down when walking.

How sizing works: Geometry, not scaling: waist radius r = (hip + 4 cm) ÷ 2π, so the waist edge is long enough to pull over your hips; the outer radius is r + length − band + hem. Each quarter is cut with 1.5 cm side seam allowances. The cutting layout packs each quarter's bounding square — nesting the quarters can save fabric on wide bolts.
