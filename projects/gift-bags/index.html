Project stages

About this project

Replace paper wrapping with bags that get used again and again. One folded rectangle, two side seams, a casing and a ribbon. Change the size for anything from jewellery to a bottle.

Techniques you'll use

  • Pressing
  • Backstitching
  • Seam allowances & right sides together
  • Topstitching
  • Making a casing

Safety

  • Keep fingers clear of the needle; switch off before changing needles or feet.
  • Irons are hot — stand upright when not in use and keep cords tidy.
  • Close rotary cutters and keep scissors pointed down when walking.

Size & customisation

Choose a size or set your own dimensions. Cutting sizes, fabric amounts and step measurements update together.

How sizing works: The bag is one rectangle folded at the bottom: width + 2 seam allowances by 2 × (height + casing + turn-under). Casing depth stays 2.5 cm; ribbon length is 2 × width + 20 cm.
