About this project

A small, practical introduction to quilting and binding. Insulated batting protects your hands; a grid of quilting holds the layers together, and a double-fold binding finishes the edges.

Techniques you'll use

  • Pressing
  • Turning gap & ladder stitch
  • Quilting the layers
  • Double-fold binding

Safety

  • Keep fingers clear of the needle; switch off before changing needles or feet.
  • Irons are hot — stand upright when not in use and keep cords tidy.
  • Close rotary cutters and keep scissors pointed down when walking.
