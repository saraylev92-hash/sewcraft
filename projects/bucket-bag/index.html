Project stages

About this project

A circular base and a tube body make a soft, roomy bucket. You'll learn to sew a curve to a straight edge evenly, install eyelets and make a drawstring channel between outer and lining.

Techniques you'll use

  • Pressing
  • Seam allowances & right sides together
  • Edgestitching
  • Topstitching
  • Lining a bag
  • Attaching straps
  • Turning gap & ladder stitch
  • Making a casing
  • Using fusible interfacing
  • Sewing curves
  • Staystitching
  • Installing eyelets

Safety

  • Keep fingers clear of the needle; switch off before changing needles or feet.
  • Irons are hot — stand upright when not in use and keep cords tidy.
  • Close rotary cutters and keep scissors pointed down when walking.
