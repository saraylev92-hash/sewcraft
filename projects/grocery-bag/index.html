About this project

The classic reusable 'T-shirt' bag: handles are part of the pattern, every seam is enclosed and it folds up small enough for a pocket. A great way to practise French seams and narrow hems on curves.

Techniques you'll use

  • Double-fold hem
  • French seams
  • Sewing curves

Safety

  • Keep fingers clear of the needle; switch off before changing needles or feet.
  • Irons are hot — stand upright when not in use and keep cords tidy.
  • Close rotary cutters and keep scissors pointed down when walking.
