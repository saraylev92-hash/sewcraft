Project stages

About this project

Three rectangles and straight seams make this the quickest route to a professional-looking home accessory. The overlapping 'envelope' back holds the insert without any zipper or buttons. Enter your insert size and the pattern recalculates.

Techniques you'll use

  • Pre-washing fabric
  • Pressing
  • Zigzag stitch
  • Backstitching
  • Seam allowances & right sides together
  • Edgestitching
  • Clipping corners & curves
  • Double-fold hem
  • Finishing raw edges

Safety

  • Keep fingers clear of the needle; switch off before changing needles or feet.
  • Irons are hot — stand upright when not in use and keep cords tidy.
  • Close rotary cutters and keep scissors pointed down when walking.
