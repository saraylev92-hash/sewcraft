Project stages

About this project

Front and back are each one piece cut on the fold, with the short sleeves built in — no setting in sleeves. A drafted scoop neck is finished with bias binding. Sized from your bust measurement with generous ease.

Techniques you'll use

  • Pre-washing fabric
  • Clipping corners & curves
  • Double-fold hem
  • Finishing raw edges
  • Double-fold binding
  • Sewing curves
  • Staystitching

Safety

  • Keep fingers clear of the needle; switch off before changing needles or feet.
  • Irons are hot — stand upright when not in use and keep cords tidy.
  • Close rotary cutters and keep scissors pointed down when walking.
