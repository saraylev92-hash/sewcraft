Project stages

About this project

Your first zipper, done the easy way. The sandwich method attaches outer, zipper and lining in one seam, and fabric tabs give clean ends. Boxed corners make it stand up on the counter. Great for makeup, pencils or chargers.

Techniques you'll use

  • Pressing
  • Backstitching
  • Seam allowances & right sides together
  • Edgestitching
  • Topstitching
  • Boxed corners
  • Clipping corners & curves
  • Turning gap & ladder stitch
  • Installing a zipper (sandwich method)
  • Using fusible interfacing

Safety

  • Keep fingers clear of the needle; switch off before changing needles or feet.
  • Irons are hot — stand upright when not in use and keep cords tidy.
  • Close rotary cutters and keep scissors pointed down when walking.
