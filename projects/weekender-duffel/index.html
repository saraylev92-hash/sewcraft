Project stages

About this project

An advanced bag that combines everything: fusible fleece for structure, a zipper panel, webbing handles running under the bag, and end panels sewn in with pivoted corners. Allow a full weekend.

Techniques you'll use

  • Seam allowances & right sides together
  • Edgestitching
  • Topstitching
  • Lining a bag
  • Attaching straps
  • Clipping corners & curves
  • Turning gap & ladder stitch
  • Installing a zipper (sandwich method)
  • Using fusible interfacing

Safety

  • Keep fingers clear of the needle; switch off before changing needles or feet.
  • Irons are hot — stand upright when not in use and keep cords tidy.
  • Close rotary cutters and keep scissors pointed down when walking.

How sizing works: The body wraps front, bottom and back (2 × height + depth). Two zipper panels make up the top depth. End panels are depth × height. All pieces carry 1 cm seam allowances; the zipper must be at least the bag length + 10 cm. Webbing length is recalculated.
