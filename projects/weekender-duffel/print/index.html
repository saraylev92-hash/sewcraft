Print pattern · Weekender Duffel Bag

  1. STEP 1

    Print the test page

    In the print dialog choose Actual size / 100% — never “Fit to page”.

  2. STEP 2

    Measure the squares

    The squares must measure exactly 5 cm and 2″. If not, check printer scaling and print again.

  3. STEP 3

    Print the pieces

    49 pages. Trim along the solid border where pages meet, then match the ◆ marks and tape.

Pieces to print

Pieces to print — all pieces are rectangles, so you can also measure them straight onto fabric.

5 × 5 cm
2″ × 2″

Check scale: measure both squares with a ruler. If they are not exact, set printing to “Actual size” / 100% and reprint. Do not cut fabric from a mis-scaled print.

Fabric

  • Outer — heavy canvas: 0.9 m of 140 cm wide (single)

Sample pattern — not yet test-sewn. Pattern rules: The body wraps front, bottom and back (2 × height + depth). Two zipper panels make up the top depth. End panels are depth × height. All pieces carry 1 cm seam allowances; the zipper must be at least the bag length + 10 cm. Webbing length is recalculated.
