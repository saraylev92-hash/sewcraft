Print pattern · Rectangle Kimono Jacket

  1. STEP 1

    Print the test page

    In the print dialog choose Actual size / 100% — never “Fit to page”.

  2. STEP 2

    Measure the squares

    The squares must measure exactly 5 cm and 2″. If not, check printer scaling and print again.

  3. STEP 3

    Print the pieces

    23 pages. Trim along the solid border where pages meet, then match the ◆ marks and tape.

Pieces to print

Pieces to print — all pieces are rectangles, so you can also measure them straight onto fabric.

Check scale: measure both squares with a ruler. If they are not exact, set printing to “Actual size” / 100% and reprint. Do not cut fabric from a mis-scaled print.

Fabric

  • Drapey woven fabric: 2.0 m of 140 cm wide (folded)

Sample pattern — not yet test-sewn. Pattern rules: Drafted, not scaled: the body circumference is bust + 24 cm ease; the back takes half of that and each front a quarter minus the band. Sleeves are a fixed 46 cm around (roomy kimono sleeve) with your length. Seam allowance stays 1.5 cm and hems 3 cm at every size.
