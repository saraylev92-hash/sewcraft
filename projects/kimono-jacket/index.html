Project stages

About this project

No curved pattern pieces: a back cut on the fold, two fronts, two sleeves and a long front band. Fit comes from your bust measurement plus generous ease, and you choose the length.

Techniques you'll use

  • Pre-washing fabric
  • Pressing
  • Seam allowances & right sides together
  • Topstitching
  • Clipping corners & curves
  • Double-fold hem
  • Finishing raw edges

Safety

  • Keep fingers clear of the needle; switch off before changing needles or feet.
  • Irons are hot — stand upright when not in use and keep cords tidy.
  • Close rotary cutters and keep scissors pointed down when walking.

Size & customisation

Choose a size or set your own dimensions. Cutting sizes, fabric amounts and step measurements update together.

How sizing works: Drafted, not scaled: the body circumference is bust + 24 cm ease; the back takes half of that and each front a quarter minus the band. Sleeves are a fixed 46 cm around (roomy kimono sleeve) with your length. Seam allowance stays 1.5 cm and hems 3 cm at every size.
