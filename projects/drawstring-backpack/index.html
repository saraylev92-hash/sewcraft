Project stages

About this project

Two cords run through casings at the top and tie into tabs at the bottom corners, so pulling the straps closes the bag. You'll learn casings, reinforced openings and edge finishing — a step up from your first tote.

Techniques you'll use

  • Pre-washing fabric
  • Pressing
  • Zigzag stitch
  • Backstitching
  • Seam allowances & right sides together
  • Edgestitching
  • Topstitching
  • Attaching straps
  • Making a casing
  • Finishing raw edges

Safety

  • Keep fingers clear of the needle; switch off before changing needles or feet.
  • Irons are hot — stand upright when not in use and keep cords tidy.
  • Close rotary cutters and keep scissors pointed down when walking.
