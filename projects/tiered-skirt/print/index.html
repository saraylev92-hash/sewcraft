Print pattern · Tiered Maxi Skirt

  1. STEP 1

    Print the test page

    In the print dialog choose Actual size / 100% — never “Fit to page”.

  2. STEP 2

    Measure the squares

    The squares must measure exactly 5 cm and 2″. If not, check printer scaling and print again.

  3. STEP 3

    Print the pieces

    25 pages. Trim along the solid border where pages meet, then match the ◆ marks and tape.

Pieces to print

Pieces to print — all pieces are rectangles, so you can also measure them straight onto fabric.

Fabric

  • Light woven fabric: 1.8 m of 140 cm wide (single)

Sample pattern — not yet test-sewn. Pattern rules: Tier 1 is hip + 20 cm around (it must pull on); tier 2 is 1.5 × that and tier 3 is 1.5 × tier 2, split across 2, 3 and 4 panels. Tier heights are 30%, 33% and 37% of your length plus casing, seam and hem allowances.
