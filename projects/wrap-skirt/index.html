Project stages

About this project

One big rectangle gathered onto a long waistband with ties. Adjustable fit, no zipper, and it suits nearly every body. Introduces gathering and a buttonhole.

Techniques you'll use

  • Pre-washing fabric
  • Edgestitching
  • Double-fold hem
  • Gathering
  • Buttonholes & buttons

Safety

  • Keep fingers clear of the needle; switch off before changing needles or feet.
  • Irons are hot — stand upright when not in use and keep cords tidy.
  • Close rotary cutters and keep scissors pointed down when walking.
