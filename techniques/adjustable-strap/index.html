Adjustable straps

D-rings and a slider for a strap you can lengthen.

Sew D-rings into fabric tabs caught in the bag's side seams. For the strap: stitch one webbing end round the slider's centre bar, take the other end through the first D-ring, back through the slider (over the centre bar), then through the second D-ring and stitch it down with a box-and-X. Seal cut webbing ends.

Video demonstration: not yet available.

How to do it

  1. 1Tabs with D-rings in the side seams.
  2. 2Webbing end round the slider bar, stitched.
  3. 3Through D-ring 1, back through the slider.
  4. 4Through D-ring 2, box-and-X stitch.

You'll need

  • D-rings
  • Strap slider
  • Webbing

Common mistakes

  • Threading the slider the wrong way — the strap slips.
  • Unsealed webbing ends fray.

Practise it in

intermediate2.5–3.5 h
