Boxed corners

Give a flat bag a base and depth.

Cut a square from each bottom corner equal to half the desired depth. After sewing the side and bottom seams, pinch the corner so the side seam sits on top of the bottom seam, and sew straight across the opening. The length of that seam equals the finished depth.

Video demonstration: not yet available.

How to do it

  1. 1Cut squares of ½ the depth from both bottom corners.
  2. 2Sew side and bottom seams, leaving the cut-outs open.
  3. 3Pinch the corner, matching the side seam to the bottom seam.
  4. 4Sew across the opening using the usual seam allowance, backstitching both ends.

You'll need

  • Ruler
  • Fabric marker
  • Scissors

Common mistakes

  • Seams not nested — the base twists.
  • Measuring the cut-out from the stitching instead of the raw edge.
