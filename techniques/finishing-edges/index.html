Finishing raw edges

Stop seams fraying inside unlined projects.

Unlined items need their seam allowances neatened. Zigzag or overlock each raw edge, use pinking shears on tightly woven cottons, or press the allowances open and zigzag each one separately.

Video demonstration: not yet available.

How to do it

  1. 1Choose a method that suits the fabric.
  2. 2Finish edges before or after sewing the seam as the pattern says.
  3. 3Press.

You'll need

  • Zigzag stitch or overlocker
  • Pinking shears (optional)

Common mistakes

  • Leaving edges raw on loosely woven linen — it frays fast.
