Attaching straps

Position and secure handles so they last.

Baste strap ends to the outer bag first with raw ends aligned to the top edge, loop hanging down. The top seam then encloses them. Topstitching around the bag top adds strength.

Video demonstration: not yet available.

How to do it

  1. 1Mark the position on both outer panels.
  2. 2Check the strap isn't twisted.
  3. 3Baste 5 mm from the top edge.
  4. 4Sew the top seam over them.

You'll need

  • Ruler
  • Clips

Common mistakes

  • Twisted strap.
  • Straps placed asymmetrically front vs back.
