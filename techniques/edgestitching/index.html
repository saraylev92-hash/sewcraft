Edgestitching

Stitching 2–3 mm from a fold for crisp edges.

Edgestitching holds folds in place and gives a professional finish. Move the needle position or use an edgestitch foot, and sew slowly, watching the fold — not the needle.

Video demonstration: not yet available.

How to do it

  1. 1Press the fold first.
  2. 2Align the fold with the foot's guide or a marked line 2–3 mm from the needle.
  3. 3Stitch at a slow, even speed.

You'll need

  • Sewing machine
  • Edgestitch foot (optional)

Common mistakes

  • Wandering off the edge — slow down and use a guide.
