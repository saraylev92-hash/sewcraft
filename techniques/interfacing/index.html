Using fusible interfacing

Add body to soft fabrics.

Place the glue (bumpy, shiny) side against the wrong side of the fabric. Cover with a damp pressing cloth and press — don't slide — for 10–15 seconds per position, overlapping positions. Let it cool flat before handling.

Video demonstration: not yet available.

How to do it

  1. 1Glue side down on the wrong side of fabric.
  2. 2Pressing cloth on top.
  3. 3Press 10–15 s per spot, lifting between spots.
  4. 4Cool flat.

You'll need

  • Iron
  • Pressing cloth

Common mistakes

  • Glue side up — it fuses to your iron.
  • Sliding the iron — bubbles and wrinkles.
