Backstitching

Lock every seam so it can't unravel.

At the start and end of each seam, sew forward 3–4 stitches, hold the reverse button and sew back over them, then continue. This knots the threads mechanically. Skip it only for basting and gathering stitches you'll remove or pull.

Video demonstration: not yet available.

How to do it

  1. 1Start 1 cm in from the edge.
  2. 2Reverse to the edge.
  3. 3Sew forward over the reversed stitches.
  4. 4Repeat at the end of the seam.

You'll need

  • Sewing machine

Common mistakes

  • Forgetting to backstitch at a turning gap — the gap tears open when turning.
