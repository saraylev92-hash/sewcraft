Pressing

Lift-and-lower ironing that sets seams and folds precisely.

Pressing is different from ironing. Instead of gliding the iron, you set it down, hold, and lift. This sets stitches, flattens seam allowances and creates crisp folds without stretching the fabric. Press every seam before crossing it with another.

Video demonstration: not yet available.

How to do it

  1. 1Set the iron to the fibre (cotton: high with steam, synthetics: low).
  2. 2Press the seam flat as sewn to 'set' the stitches.
  3. 3Open the seam allowances with your fingertip and press them open (or to one side).
  4. 4Let the fabric cool before moving it — that's when the crease sets.

You'll need

  • Iron
  • Ironing board
  • Pressing cloth (optional)

Common mistakes

  • Sliding the iron and stretching bias edges.
  • Too much heat on synthetics — shiny marks or melting.
