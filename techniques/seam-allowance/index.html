Seam allowances & right sides together

Where to stitch, and which way the fabric faces.

Most seams are sewn with the fabric right sides together so the seam is hidden after turning. The seam allowance — 1 cm in all SewCraft patterns unless stated — is the gap between the raw edge and the stitching. Your machine's throat plate has guide lines; keep the raw edge on the correct line.

Video demonstration: not yet available.

How to do it

  1. 1Lay piece A right side up.
  2. 2Lay piece B right side down on top, edges matched.
  3. 3Pin or clip perpendicular to the edge.
  4. 4Sew with the raw edge on the seam allowance guide.

You'll need

  • Pins or clips
  • Sewing machine

Common mistakes

  • Using the wrong guide line — every seam ends up too wide or too narrow.
  • Sewing wrong sides together.
