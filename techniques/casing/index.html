Making a casing

A channel for elastic or a drawstring.

Fold the raw edge under 1 cm and press, then fold again by the casing depth (elastic width + 1 cm) and press. Stitch close to the lower fold, leaving an opening to insert the elastic or exit the cord.

Video demonstration: not yet available.

How to do it

  1. 1Press 1 cm to the wrong side.
  2. 2Press again by the casing depth.
  3. 3Edgestitch along the lower fold.
  4. 4Leave a 5 cm opening for elastic (not needed for side-exit drawstrings).

You'll need

  • Iron
  • Ruler or seam gauge
  • Safety pin or bodkin

Common mistakes

  • Casing too narrow — elastic twists or won't fit.
  • Catching the elastic in the stitching.
