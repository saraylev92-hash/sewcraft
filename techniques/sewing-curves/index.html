Sewing curves

Smooth curved seams without puckers.

Sew curves slowly, guiding the fabric so the edge stays on the seam guide just in front of the needle, and stop with the needle down to pivot slightly every few stitches. After sewing, clip inward (concave) curves and notch outward (convex) curves so the seam lies flat when turned.

Video demonstration: not yet available.

How to do it

  1. 1Shorten the stitch slightly (2–2.2 mm).
  2. 2Watch the seam guide, not the needle.
  3. 3Stop with the needle down and turn the fabric a little at a time.
  4. 4Clip or notch the allowance, then press.

You'll need

  • Sewing machine
  • Small sharp scissors

Common mistakes

  • Pulling the fabric round the curve — it stretches and ripples.
  • Not clipping — the curve pulls and won't lie flat.
