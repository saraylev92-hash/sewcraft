Pre-washing fabric

Wash fabric the way you'll wash the finished item, before you cut.

Natural fibres such as cotton, linen and rayon can shrink 3–8% the first time they are washed. Washing and drying before cutting means the shrinkage happens to the yardage — not to your finished project. It also removes sizing (stiffening finish) and reveals colour bleeding.

Video demonstration: not yet available.

How to do it

  1. 1Zig-zag or overlock the cut ends so they don't fray in the machine.
  2. 2Wash at the temperature you intend to use for the finished item.
  3. 3Dry the same way (tumble or line).
  4. 4Press out creases before laying out the pattern.

You'll need

  • Washing machine
  • Iron

Common mistakes

  • Skipping pre-wash on cotton canvas — the finished bag shrinks and the lining puckers.
  • Washing dry-clean-only fabrics.
