Installing a zipper (sandwich method)

Outer + zipper + lining sewn in one pass.

For lined pouches, lay the outer right side up, the zipper face down on top with tape edge aligned to the raw edge, then the lining right side down. Sew through all layers with a zipper foot. Open the zipper partway when you reach the pull so you can sew past it in a straight line.

Video demonstration: not yet available.

How to do it

  1. 1Attach a zipper foot.
  2. 2Stack outer (right side up), zipper (face down), lining (face down).
  3. 3Sew along the edge, stopping to slide the pull out of the way.
  4. 4Press layers away from the teeth and topstitch.

You'll need

  • Zipper foot
  • Clips

Common mistakes

  • Sewing too close to the teeth — the pull jams.
  • Forgetting to open the zipper before the final seam — you can't turn the pouch.
