Double-fold binding

A folded strip that wraps and finishes a quilted edge.

Cut strips 6–6.5 cm wide, join them with diagonal seams and press in half lengthwise. Sew the raw edges to the front of the item, mitring each corner by folding the strip up at 45° and back down. Wrap the fold to the back and stitch it by hand or in the ditch from the front.

Video demonstration: not yet available.

How to do it

  1. 1Join strips with diagonal seams; press in half.
  2. 2Sew raw edges to the front.
  3. 3Mitre each corner: stop 1 seam allowance from the corner, fold up at 45°, fold down, continue.
  4. 4Wrap to the back and stitch.

You'll need

  • Rotary cutter
  • Clips
  • Hand-sewing needle

Common mistakes

  • Binding too narrow to cover the stitching on the back.
  • Stretching the binding round corners.
