French seams

A seam that encloses its own raw edges — no fraying, no overlocker.

Sew the seam first with WRONG sides together using a narrow allowance, trim it, then fold right sides together and sew again just wider than the trimmed allowance. The raw edges end up sealed inside a neat little tube. Ideal for pillowcases, unlined bags and sheer fabrics.

Video demonstration: not yet available.

How to do it

  1. 1Wrong sides together, sew at 0.5 cm.
  2. 2Trim the allowance to 3 mm and press.
  3. 3Fold right sides together, rolling the seam to the edge; press.
  4. 4Sew again at 0.7 cm, enclosing the raw edges.

You'll need

  • Sewing machine
  • Iron

Common mistakes

  • Starting right sides together — the finished seam ends up on the outside.
  • Not trimming enough — whiskers poke through the second seam.
