Clipping corners & curves

Reduce bulk so corners turn out sharp.

Cut diagonally across the seam allowance 2 mm from the stitching at each corner. This removes bulk so the corner can form a point when turned.

Video demonstration: not yet available.

How to do it

  1. 1Sew and backstitch.
  2. 2Cut diagonally across the corner allowance.
  3. 3Don't cut through the stitches.
  4. 4Turn and gently push the corner out with a blunt tool.

You'll need

  • Sharp small scissors
  • Point turner or chopstick

Common mistakes

  • Snipping through the stitching — a hole appears when turned.
