Double-fold hem

Enclose the raw edge in two folds.

Press the edge under once (usually 1 cm), then again by the hem depth, and stitch close to the inner fold. The raw edge is fully enclosed, so no other finishing is needed.

Video demonstration: not yet available.

How to do it

  1. 1Press the first fold.
  2. 2Press the second fold.
  3. 3Pin and stitch near the inner fold from the wrong side.

You'll need

  • Iron
  • Seam gauge

Common mistakes

  • Uneven folds — measure with a seam gauge as you press.
