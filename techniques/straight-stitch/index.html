Straight stitch

The everyday machine stitch for seams.

A straight stitch is a single line of stitches. Most seams use a length of 2.5 mm; use 3–3.5 mm on thick fabrics and for topstitching, and the longest length (4–5 mm) for basting.

Video demonstration: not yet available.

How to do it

  1. 1Lower the needle into the fabric at the seam allowance guide.
  2. 2Lower the presser foot.
  3. 3Guide the fabric with the edge against the seam guide — don't pull it.
  4. 4Backstitch at both ends.

You'll need

  • Sewing machine

Common mistakes

  • Pulling the fabric through instead of letting the feed dogs move it.
  • Watching the needle instead of the seam guide.
