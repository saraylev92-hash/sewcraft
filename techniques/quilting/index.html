Quilting the layers

Stitching through top, batting and backing so they act as one.

A quilt 'sandwich' is backing (right side down), batting, then the top (right side up). Smooth each layer, pin every 10 cm with safety pins, and quilt from the centre outwards so any excess is pushed to the edges. A walking foot feeds all layers evenly; lengthen the stitch to about 3 mm.

Video demonstration: not yet available.

How to do it

  1. 1Smooth and tape the backing wrong side up.
  2. 2Add batting, then the top.
  3. 3Pin every 10 cm.
  4. 4Quilt from the centre outwards with a 3 mm stitch.

You'll need

  • Walking foot (recommended)
  • Curved safety pins

Common mistakes

  • Quilting from one edge to the other — the layers drift and pucker.
  • Too few pins.
