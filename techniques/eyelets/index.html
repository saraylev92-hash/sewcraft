Installing eyelets

Metal grommets for drawstrings and laces.

Mark the centre, cut a hole slightly smaller than the eyelet's inner diameter, push the tube part through from the right side, place the washer on the back and set it with the setting tool and a firm hammer blow on a hard surface. Add interfacing or a scrap behind thin fabric.

Video demonstration: not yet available.

How to do it

  1. 1Mark and cut a small hole.
  2. 2Tube from the front, washer on the back.
  3. 3Use the setting tool on a hard surface.
  4. 4Hammer firmly 2–3 times.

You'll need

  • Eyelet kit and setting tool
  • Hammer
  • Hole punch or small scissors

Common mistakes

  • Hole too big — the eyelet pulls out.
  • Setting on a soft surface.

Practise it in

intermediate3–4.5 h
