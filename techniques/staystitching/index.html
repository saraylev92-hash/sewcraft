Staystitching

A line of stitching that stops curved edges stretching.

Necklines, waistlines and other curves cut partly on the bias stretch as soon as you handle them. Straight after cutting, sew a single line of regular stitching just inside the seam allowance (usually 1–1.2 cm from the edge), working from each end towards the centre. It stays inside the finished seam.

Video demonstration: not yet available.

How to do it

  1. 1Do it right after cutting.
  2. 2Stitch 2–3 mm inside the seam line.
  3. 3Sew from the shoulder towards the centre on each side.
  4. 4Don't pull the fabric.

You'll need

  • Sewing machine

Common mistakes

  • Staystitching after the curve has already stretched.
