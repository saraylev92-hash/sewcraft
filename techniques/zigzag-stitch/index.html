Zigzag stitch

Side-to-side stitch for neatening raw edges and sewing elastic.

Zigzag stretches, which makes it the right choice for elastic, and when sewn along a raw edge it wraps the threads to stop fraying. A width of 4–5 mm and length of 2–2.5 mm suits most edge finishing.

Video demonstration: not yet available.

How to do it

  1. 1Set width 4–5 mm, length 2–2.5 mm.
  2. 2Position the fabric so the right swing of the needle lands just off the raw edge.
  3. 3Sew slowly along the edge.

You'll need

  • Sewing machine

Common mistakes

  • Sewing too far in from the edge so threads still fray.
  • Stitch too short on thin fabric — it tunnels and puckers.
