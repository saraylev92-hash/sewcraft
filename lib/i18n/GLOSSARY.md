# Hebrew UI glossary (draft, waiting on Saray's review)

Use these terms everywhere so the interface and the guides read the same way.
If you're unsure about a term, add it here with a `?` instead of making one up.

## Voice
- Address the reader with **plural imperative**: "בחרו", "גזרו", "תפרו". It's gender-neutral and friendly.
- Keep it short, warm and plain, the way an experienced sewing teacher talks. Avoid literal translation.
- Measurements: "ס״מ" in Hebrew text. The cm/in toggle and the diagrams stay as they are. Use gershayim (״), not two apostrophes.
- Brand: "SewCraft" stays in Latin letters.
- Numbers come out in digits, inside the sentence: "5 שלבים".
